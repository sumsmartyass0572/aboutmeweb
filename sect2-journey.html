~ II. The Journey ~

So how did I get here in the first place? Programming is really hard to master, but let me tell you - I genuinely learned one of the hardest skill at a young age. Let me show you the journey. From start to finish.

Year 2020-2021: Entering Primary School

Yes, this journey started all the way back when I was about.. 7 years old (aka just entering primary school). In my school, we had a variety of co-curricular activities (CCA) such as badminton, basketball, drama, and et cetera. While I was deciding on what CCA I should join, something stood out for young me - coding and game development. So, I joined coding at the price of Rp ~1,000,000. That's an absurd price for something I would do only on Wednesday.

In our first meetings, our CCA teacher (who I forgot his name, yes he..) taught us Scratch. To those who don't know, Scratch is a block-based coding website - meaning it is suitable for people who're getting started with programming! I was enjoying this period, because for my whole life before this moment I dreamt, and dreamt of making my own games. And now in this moment, it was a dream come true.

Year 2022-2025: "Created a Scratch Account!"

A few years after I started coding in 1st grade, I eventually made a Scratch account. By the good ol' name Ax1182. Yes, I am aware of using half of my name in the account's name, but no one's going to know who I am! From there, I started getting better at coding, not just from the CCA lessons I'm taking. I learned algorithms that make interactions look much smoother. I learned how to make text counters with the help of tutorials, especially from griffpatch. Long story short: he's considered as one of the best Scratch coders!

However, gaining fame was very slow. Usually you would have to advertise this through.. say social media. The catch is, I don't have any social media account! So I just prayed some people saw my works and that was pretty much it... until griffpatch uploaded a tutorial on making Geometry Dash. This happened around 2024, when I was 4th grade. So I did the obvious move, to do the tutorial, and it ended up very well made; that's how good griffpatch is! I took this opportunity 1 step further by uploading my project in a studio. And it did end up blowing up to 300+ views! Though very miniscule compared to the most famous projects with millions of views, I was still proud of this achievement.

Year 2025-Present: A New Era of Programming

This is where problems arise. Finding a very unique game idea was a complete struggle for me. I kept on thinking of clickers, platformers, and well.. other unoriginal genres. But that doesn't mean I completely quit on programming on Scratch.

Now in secondary school, I decided to continue my programming journey in another extracurricular activity named Web Development. And this is where I am right now - making my own website! This type of programming is significantly harder compared to Scratch. However this does mean there is more potential to do new stuff, as you are quite literally making a website! Sometimes, I still code abit on Scratch for games. Websites? That's for later as I haven't learned JavaScript.

How has programming benefited me?

Programming has a lot of benefits in my opinion. For one, programming helps build greater logic thinking, like how you would respond if any incident happens. Usually you would use this skill in programming, obviously.

Secondly, I think programming can increase brain strength and memory. For instance, greater brain memory means that you know how to do things more quickly, without having to remember as hard on what you forgot. Or to know how to make things more efficient. Basically, it makes you understand more the longer you learn - like the more you study the more you understand a topic!

And that is my programming journey! Over these years programming not only made me overall smarter, but also made me more capable of solving problems.
