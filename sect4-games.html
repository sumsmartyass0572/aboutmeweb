What advice do I have?

Programming is hard, and so much other things. The only way I'd think you could do to master programming (or other skills) is to just keep practicing. There's no real strategy. But I could give some advice. Keep in mind that, some of these advice may not help too much depending on your personality and mentality. But regardless here are some advice I got - hope they help!

  1. Try New Things!
    In my programming journey I learned many different things. Why? Because I had the courage to, try new ways of coding! In the very first year I was just getting started, following my CCA teacher. But by the third or fourth year I started learning algorithms. Yes, I still use tutorials, but that's okay! Everyone starts somewhere, and I don't expect anyone instantly knowing something before learning it. So keep trying new things, and you will learn a lot more things.

  2. Don't be discouraged, because of mistakes.
    It's easy to be discouraged because you did something wrong. Or someone is telling you:
    "hey, just stop trying. you're probably not suited for this."
    Ignore these hate messages. It's their opinion anyway, so follow your dreams and passions! Words can't stop you afterall!

  3. Have Discipline
    Motivation can run out fast, because of distractions. So I would suggest on having more discipline during your journey! It could be as easy as keeping yourself away from your phone, and tadaa! You suddenly work for like 2 hours, no breaks! However that doesn't mean you shouldn't take breaks. Don't push yourself to your limits, and take breaks when needed!
