~ III. Future Plans ~

To this day, I still enjoy programming as a hobby. What plans do I have for the future, using my programming knowledge? We'll talk a bit into that here.
There are two main things that I plan to do:

1. Make Games for a Living

Let's be honest, most of us have already played a game before, and we usually find them fun! So in the future, I plan to make games, not ones that are pure slop, rather actually come up with a unique concept. And not just Scratch games that I'm good at making, probably games made in softwares like Unity, or Gobot!

And to top it all off, I pretty much grew up making games when coding! Because I've never truly tried on coding something new back then, it just made me very used to and familiar to code games.

But, why games exactly?
The thing is, I personally think that games have alot more potential than websites. Like this website? It's pretty ordinary but compared to games, there's so much more you could do! You could make bossfights, clickers, or even a multiplayer game! This is what I mean by "more potential" - there's just so much more things to make, and creativity's endless!

However I have to address some problems. For one, games are usually made BIG - to keep players hooked and so that they do not speedrun the game in minutes. Because that would be boring! And because of this, there's alot of room for error when coding the game itself. I kind of feel game developers now, it sort of irritates them, but probably gets them raging over time! And second, it's pretty hard to get, even keep motivation. I, myself face this same problem - because when making games you'd think of making something unique. But now, it's hard to make a new concept. And we're not including the coding part yet, because I tend to forget about passion projects because of their size.

2. Websites for Business

The other choice that I could make in the future is, using my newly-learned website-coding skill for business purposes. Websites afterall, offer a good amount of elements to make a proper business website, like textboxes for forums, hyperlinks for travelling through different websites, and other elements.

Websites are also not that hard to decorate with CSS! If you get used to them.. And websites also offer actual functionality with JavaScript, however I have no experience in JavaScript at all, but will soon be learning that! So for now I can't say for sure about JavaScript's difficulty.

Those are my plans for the future. They could change and possibly cancelled, because well.. business and time problems I suppose. But the future is yet to come! And we are yet to reach there! The final part is all about my games, which you could go there by clicking here.
